## Full description (4000 char max)

Voices of the Founders brings seven American Founding Fathers to life — George Washington, Thomas Jefferson,
Alexander Hamilton, James Madison, Benjamin Franklin, John Adams, and John Jay — through interactive timelines,
quizzes, and games covering the full story of the American Revolution and founding era.

FEATURES

📜 Interactive Timelines
Explore each founder's life through key historical events, from their early years to their lasting legacy.

❓ 244-Question Quiz Bank
Test your knowledge with questions covering each founder's life plus the full sweep of the founding era — the
Road to Revolution, the Declaration of Independence, the Constitutional Convention, the Federalist/Anti-Federalist
ratification debate, and the early presidencies. Includes primary-source questions with real excerpts from the
Declaration, the Federalist Papers, and more. Questions adapt to focus on what you haven't mastered yet.

💬 Quote Matching Game
Match famous quotes to the founder who said them.

🎯 Daily Challenge
Answer a few questions each day to build a streak and earn XP.

🎡 Daily Spin Wheel
Spin once a day for a free bonus of coins and XP.

⚡ Quiz Power-Ups
Use coins for a 50/50 (eliminate two wrong answers) or Skip a tough question.

📊 Progress Tracking
See your mastery level for each founder, review past mistakes, and track your best scores.

🔔 Optional Daily Reminder
Turn on a daily notification to keep your streak alive.

No accounts. No ads. No internet connection required. Everything stays on your device.
