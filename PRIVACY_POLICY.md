# Privacy Policy — Voices of the Founders

_Last updated: 2026-07-21_

Voices of the Founders ("the App") is developed and published by WithOn9. This policy explains what data the App
collects and how it is used.

## Summary

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no ads, and
no network access of any kind — everything the App does happens entirely on your device.

## What the App stores, and where

All data below is stored **only on your device**, using Android's local app storage (DataStore). It is never sent
anywhere. Uninstalling the App deletes it permanently.

- Quiz progress: which questions you've answered, your accuracy per Founding Father, and your wrong-answer history
  (so you can review mistakes).
- Game state: XP, streak count, coin balance, best scores, and daily challenge/spin progress.
- Preferences: sound and music volume settings, and whether the daily reminder notification is enabled.

## Permissions the App requests

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)** — only used to show the optional daily reminder you can enable in
  Settings. If you don't enable this, the App never requests or uses notification access at all.
- **Vibration** — used only for haptic feedback when you answer a question, so you can feel the response.

No other device permissions (location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, etc.) are requested or used.

## Third parties

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. No data leaves your
device under any circumstance.

## Children's privacy

The App contains no user-generated content, accounts, chat, or data collection of any kind, and is safe for users
of any age.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, an updated version will be published at the same location and the "Last
updated" date above will be revised accordingly.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to WithOn9.
